# 🚀 Data Science & Business Intelligence Job Simulations Portfolio

Welcome to my professional data science portfolio. This repository houses three high-impact machine learning and business intelligence projects completed during virtual industry simulations with **British Airways**, **Boston Consulting Group (BCG X)**, and **Tata Group** via Forage [1, 0.1.6]. 

As a Level 200 Data Science student at Kumasi Technical University, these projects demonstrate my ability to process over 70,000 raw transactional data points, build automated feature pipelines, deploy ensemble classifiers, and deliver top-down executive insights that pass the consulting "so-what?" test.

---

## 📊 1. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) — Retail Expansion Analytics
* **Core Skills:** Dashboard Architecture, Data Transformation, Data Cleaning, Information Synthesis.
* **The Business Challenge:** Acted as a BI Consultant preparing a steering committee brief for the corporate CEO and CMO to determine regional expansion targets for an international e-commerce brand [2, 0.1.6].
* **The Technical Solution:** Built a robust data purification pipeline inside Power Query to filter out negative refund records and pricing errors. Developed a 4-tab interactive Power BI (`.pbix`) dashboard mapping 2011 time-series line trends, customer revenue concentration ladders, and global geographic choropleth maps.
* **Strategic Business Impact:** Isolated France, Germany, and Ireland as prime, high-yield geographic targets for expansion capital, allowing leadership to allocate infrastructure budget efficiently while protecting core domestic profit margins.

## 🔋 2. BCG X — Predictive Energy Churn Analytics & Feature Engineering
* **Core Skills:** Advanced Feature Engineering, Random Forest Classification, Class Imbalance Mitigation [1, 0.1.6].
* **The Business Challenge:** Investigated a costly 10% portfolio customer churn rate across the high-value Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) segment of an energy utility provider (PowerCo) [1, 0.1.6].
* **The Technical Solution:** Formulated a 5-step data science lifecycle in Python using Jupyter Notebooks [1, 0.1.6]. Engineered specialized price volatility features tracking maximum and minimum tariff movements across the calendar year. Trained an ensemble Random Forest Classifier using Scikit-Learn to evaluate feature contribution weights [1, 0.1.6].
* **Strategic Business Impact:** Disproved management's original hypothesis by proving that price spikes alone do not drive churn. Delivered an 80.77% Targeted Churn Precision score, enabling PowerCo to protect profit margins by rolling out defensive loyalty discounts exclusively to verified at-risk accounts.

## ✈️ 3. British Airways — Customer Predictive Modeling
* **Core Skills:** Web Scraping, Data Preprocessing, Supervised Machine Learning, Model Evaluation.
* **The Business Challenge:** Programmed algorithms to analyze consumer behavior data to accurately predict which online users would complete a flight booking conversion.
* **The Technical Solution:** Extracted and preprocessed 50,000 rows of raw digital customer browsing session histories using Python (Pandas/NumPy). Trained and evaluated an ensemble Random Forest model to isolate specific behavioral intent variables.
* **Strategic Business Impact:** Achieved a stable 85.34% prediction accuracy and isolated "Purchase Lead Time" and "Trip Stay Duration" as the primary drivers of purchase intent, providing the marketing division with a 56.00% customer targeting precision profile to optimize global digital ad spend.
